Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use Liora, why it is used and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may include your name, email address, phone number, country, account-registration details, payment reference and information you choose to provide when contacting support.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your registration record.
  • To display relevant account and payment information.
  • To review payment confirmations and respond to support requests.
  • To investigate errors, protect the website and maintain its operation.
  • To meet applicable legal and record-keeping obligations.

3. Storage and security

Information submitted through the website may be stored using third-party infrastructure providers, including Firebase services. Those providers apply their own security measures. Data transmitted using HTTPS is encrypted in transit where supported by the browser and service.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website, network or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We do not claim that every item of information is end-to-end encrypted.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, store records, provide authentication or deliver support. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect users and the service.

5. Retention and your choices

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including account administration, payment checks, dispute handling and legal obligations. You may contact support to ask about access, correction or deletion of your information, subject to records we are required or permitted to retain.

6. Cookies and local browser storage

The website may use browser storage to remember registration or session-related information and support features such as countdowns. Clearing browser data may remove information stored locally on your device. Browser storage is separate from records stored on Firebase.

7. Children's privacy

Liora is not intended for children who are below the age at which they can legally enter into the relevant agreement in their location. Do not submit a child's personal information without appropriate legal authority.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our website or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact Liora through the official support channel provided on the website.

Before publishing, add a monitored privacy contact address or support channel and confirm that the practices described here match your actual data handling and retention settings.
