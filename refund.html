Refund Policy

We want you to understand your options if you decide you no longer wish to use Liora.

1. Your right to request a refund

If you have paid an activation or service fee and decide that you no longer wish to use the website or the related service, you may submit a refund request. Please contact Liora through the official support channel shown on the website.

2. How to request a refund

  1. Contact support and clearly state that you wish to cancel and request a refund.
  2. Provide the name and contact details associated with your registration.
  3. Provide your payment reference or transaction receipt so the payment can be identified.
  4. Do not send your password, PIN or one-time banking codes.

3. Processing time

Once your request and payment have been verified and approved, the refund will be processed within 7 to 14 days. Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to show the funds in your account.

If we need further information to verify the transaction, we will contact you through the details you provided. The processing period begins once the required information has been received and the request is approved.

4. Refund method

Where possible, refunds will be sent back through the original payment method. If that is not possible, support may request verified alternative account details belonging to the person who made the payment.

5. Questions or delays

If you have not received an update within the stated processing period, contact the official support channel and include your refund reference. We will review the status and provide an update.

Keep your payment receipt and any refund correspondence until the matter is resolved.
